# Portfolio — all pages

Scope: the whole site (Home, About, Projects, Qasa POS, Contact) in English, Arabic and Kurdish. Mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: local business owners who need a website, a point-of-sale system or a repair, and employers deciding on a job or internship, weighted equally. Both should see real work, believe he delivers, and contact him. The owner asked for a fresh look (2026-10-08): not a template, not technical, not plain, and no moving background.

Action: send him a job ticket on WhatsApp (or email, or call). Proof: screenshots of the two client sites and Qasa, and the list of delivered work. Constraints: the content decisions in PRODUCT.md (no status, no location, no skills list, and the rest).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is Mustafa's job-ticket book. Every piece of work, whatever its kind, is the same ticket, and the next blank ticket is made out to the visitor. It refuses the developer portfolio's hero-over-card-grid and its terminal costume.

OWN-WORLD: Carbon-paper indigo ground. Sheets in white, canary and pink stock, coded by kind of job. Pre-printed form-blue labels and rules, numbering-machine red ticket numbers, blue-black ink, perforation rows. Archivo in three widths: condensed caps for labels, normal for text, expanded heavy for printed headers. Noto Kufi Arabic headers over Noto Sans Arabic text for Arabic and Kurdish.

STORY: The visitor sees who takes the job and what he takes on, reads past tickets with real screenshots, believes any job gets the same care, and sends a ticket of their own.

FIRST VIEWPORT (as built and reviewed): One white ticket spanning the column on the indigo ground, with the pink copy turned behind it. Its printed header is the name across the sheet in expanded black caps, the role and the offer under it, "Job ticket No. 011" at the inline end. Below, ruled form rows: Customer, Job (four tick boxes), Details, Taken by. The canary copy is pulled out from under the ticket and shows the message as it is written; the primary action, Send on WhatsApp, sits on that copy and closes the set.

FORM: The job ticket, candidate 3 of 7; seed key 975997a0. Signature interaction: ticking a job draws the cross in pen, and what is written comes through on the canary copy as the message that will be sent. Motion grammar: sheets feed in from below once; nothing loops.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Finish record (2026-10-08): reviewed; every build-side fix scored resolved. One item stays open and is the owner's: seven of the ten jobs have no picture, and only real photos or exports supplied with his clients' agreement can close it. DESIGN.md and its sidecar were rewritten from the built site. The five screenshots carry their origin.

Unresolved: a LinkedIn link and a photo are not confirmed by the owner and stay out.
